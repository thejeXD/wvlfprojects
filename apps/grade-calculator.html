Information

Your data is private and is never sent to a server. This application uses your browser's localStorage for essential functionality, which is different from tracking cookies.

  • Settings: Your preferences (like theme and grade weights) are saved for convenience.
  • Grade Data: Your subjects and grades are saved so you don't lose your work when you refresh or close the page.

You can clear all this data at any time by clearing your browser's cache and site data for this page.
