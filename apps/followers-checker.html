How to Use

  1. Go to Instagram Settings → Security → Download Data
  2. Request a download of your data
  3. Once received, extract the ZIP file
  4. Open the following.html and followers.html files
  5. Copy everything and paste here - dates and URLs will be automatically removed!

You can also manually copy usernames from your Instagram app:

  1. Go to your profile
  2. Tap on "Following" or "Followers"
  3. Copy usernames one by one
  4. Paste them into the respective field (one per line)
